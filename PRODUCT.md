## Platform

web

## Users

- Primary: potential clients evaluating Wilgrey for software projects.
- Secondary: recruiters and employers considering Wilgrey for a role.

## Product Purpose

Present Wilgrey Ravelo Cruz's work, skills, experience, and services so visitors can assess his fit and contact him about a project or job opportunity. Clients should leave confident that their work would be handled responsibly.

## Positioning

Wilgrey presents himself as a software professional adapted to current tools and practices: backend and frontend development with .NET 10 and React, broader technical abilities, practical use of AI, and a personal enthusiasm for creating software.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse the Spanish-language portfolio's introduction, career timeline, services, technologies, and project examples. They can follow project links, view the résumé, or make direct contact through WhatsApp, email, or LinkedIn. There is no on-site account or checkout flow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The site is a web portfolio built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and Tailwind CSS.
- Future content should lead with potential clients while remaining useful for hiring decisions.
- The current site describes .NET Core 8 in several places. The owner now identifies .NET 10 and React as core skills; reconcile those references when updating public copy, preserving historical project and role details where accurate.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name Wilgrey Ravelo Cruz and Spanish visitor-facing copy.
- Convey professionalism, responsibility, enthusiasm for software creation, and comfort with modern tools, including AI. Ground these qualities in real work rather than unsupported promises.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner confirmed in September 2026 that he currently works at CoopHispánica as a junior full stack developer using .NET and React, developing banking systems, financial cores, card systems, credit systems, and other financial software. Describe this role without assuming a specific .NET version or inventing project outcomes.

`data.tsx` contains service descriptions, career history, project entries, contact channels, and outbound links. `public/` holds project and profile imagery; `Cv/` holds a résumé PDF. Keep claims tied to these materials or later information confirmed by the owner. Do not invent testimonials, client results, or credentials.

## Product Principles

1. Help a potential client understand what Wilgrey can build and how to contact him.
2. Show both backend and frontend capability through concrete work and experience.
3. Present AI use as a practical part of software work, supported by real examples.
4. Make the same evidence useful to recruiters without displacing the client focus.
